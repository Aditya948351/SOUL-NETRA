The Ground Reality & Problem

Why annual paper audits fail

Government audits (CAG 2024–2026) revealed that institutions operate in an 11-month fraud window between infrequent annual visits. Advance notice allows corrupt NGOs to stage students, falsify manual registers, and manipulate attendance photos.

Key Insight: 94.53% fake bank data in PMKVY and ₹1.62 Cr paid to zero-student ghost hostels in Maharashtra highlight that passive monitoring is broken.

Result: 80% reduction in ghost facilities, 60% faster grant release for compliant NGOs, and zero privacy compromise under DPDP Act 2023.

Deep Learning Video Anomaly Detection (VAD)

97%+ Area Under Curve (AUC)
  • Spatio-Temporal Autoencoders & Transformers: Processes edge CCTV frames to learn normal motion and occupancy distributions in classrooms, dormitories, and dining halls.
  • Zero-Occupancy & Ghost Detection: Flags institutions where registered enrollments are 50+ but CCTV average occupancy stays near 0 for consecutive days.
  • Camera Health & Anti-Tamper: Automatically detects camera rotation toward ceilings, tape over lenses, or loop injections via SSIM disparity.

Sequential Anti-Spoofing MFA Pipeline

96.67% Authenticated Accuracy
  • Step 1 - Polygon Geofence Lock: Inspector/beneficiary must be physically within verified WGS84 boundary (cross-checked via Cell Tower & BSSID).
  • Step 2 - Hardware Integrity: Disallows rooted devices, Android mock location providers, or developer emulators.
  • Step 3 - Active & Passive Liveness: Performs micro-texture analysis to reject 2D paper photos and dynamic motion prompts (blink, head nod) to stop screen replays.
