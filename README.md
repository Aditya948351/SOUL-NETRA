## 🎯 Executive Overview
**SOUL-NETRA** is an AI-powered, centralized real-time monitoring and inspection platform built for the Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment (MoSJE). It transforms passive CCTV feeds, mobile biometric inspections, and Grant-in-Aid (GIA) verification into an active intelligence ecosystem that **eliminates ghost hostels, prevents attendance fraud, automates surprise inspections, and guarantees 100% DPDP Act 2023 privacy compliance**.

1. **📊 Overview & Key Metrics Dashboard**:
   - Visual KPI cards detailing CAG audit figures (₹1.62 Cr ghost hostels, 94.53% invalid bank records in PMKVY, 2.56 Lakh duplicate face photos in MGNREGA).
   - Core research benchmarks (97%+ AUC in Video Anomaly Detection, 96.67% Sequential MFA accuracy).

2. **⚖️ CAG Audit Evidence & Ground Truth**:
   - Deep-dive case studies covering Maharashtra Ghost Hostels, PMKVY Data Fraud, Rajasthan MGNREGA photo duplication, and Kalyana Lakshmi scheme audit findings.

3. **⚠️ The 10 Systemic Monitoring Gaps**:
   - Detailed diagnosis of current manual/annual inspection failures and SOUL-NETRA's direct active solution for each bottleneck.

4. **🏛️ DoSJE Scheme Ecosystem**:
   - Matrix mapping SC/ST Hostels, De-addiction centres (IRCAs/DDACs/ATFs), Senior Citizen Homes (AVYAY), and Skill training centres (SMILE/PM-DAKSH).

5. **🏗️ 6-Layer Multi-Tier System Architecture**:
   - **Layer 1:** Edge & Field Ingestion (RTSP streams, mobile inspector app, beneficiary portal)
   - **Layer 2:** AI Video Anomaly Engine (YOLOv10 headcount, empty premises detection >97% AUC)
   - **Layer 3:** Sequential MFA & Anti-Spoofing (Dynamic geofencing + 3D active liveness)
   - **Layer 4:** Algorithmic Surprise Dispatcher (Risk-weighted 2-hour JIT assignment)
   - **Layer 5:** Central XAI Command Dashboard (Risk heatmaps & explainable anomaly cards)
   - **Layer 6:** Citizen Verification & Grievance (Random direct video calls & encrypted reporting)

6. **🧠 AI Video Anomaly & Interactive Threat Simulator**:
   - Live interactive simulator testing how headcount discrepancy, biometric variance, and camera health dynamically calculate institutional threat scores and trigger automated surprise visits.

7. **📚 State-of-the-Art Literature Review**:
   - Table summarizing 73+ deep learning studies, 2024–2026 IEEE/Springer/MDPI/Frontiers papers with direct links and relevance annotations.

8. **📊 Existing Systems Comparative Benchmark**:
   - Comprehensive matrix comparing SOUL-NETRA against Videonetics CCTV360, Gujarat Police AI CCTV, NMMS (NREGA), GIA Inspection App, and MoSJE Portal.

9. **🛡️ DPDP Act 2023 & Security Threat Matrix**:
   - Edge-based face blurring, sovereign cloud hosting, 30-90 day auto-purge policies, and defense vectors against deepfakes, mock GPS, and offline rural environments.

10. **🎤 SIH Hackathon Pitch Deck (12 Slides Blueprint)**:

11. **🔗 155+ Hyperlinked Reference Library**:
